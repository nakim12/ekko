How it's tested

Written down before the data is read.

The hypotheses and the analysis plan are committed publicly before any outcome is looked at, so the answer can't be tuned after the fact.

  1. Each player against themselves.Comparing you to your own baseline means "worse players lose more" can't explain the result.
  2. Early-game performance.Gold difference at 14 minutes, measured before most of a game is decided.
  3. Team strength, not vibes.Loser's queue is tested on the skill gap between your team and the enemy team, against what your rank predicts.
  4. Selection handled.Players who stop after a loss aren't the same as players who keep queueing, and the analysis accounts for it.
  5. Null results count.If loser's queue isn't real, that's the finding.
