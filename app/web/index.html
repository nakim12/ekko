Ekko · ranked League of Legends

Is it tilt, or is it the queue?

Every ranked player has had the losing streak that felt rigged. Ekko tests both explanations with real match data: did you play worse after losing, or did matchmaking hand you weaker teammates?

Collecting data. Findings coming soon.

Three questions

Q1

Tilt

After a loss, do players perform worse in their next game than their own usual level?

Q2

Loser's queue

After a streak, are your teammates weaker relative to your opponents than your rank predicts?

Q3

Streaks

Are win and loss streaks actually longer than chance would produce?

How it's tested

  • Pre-registered. Hypotheses and methods are committed publicly before any results are looked at.
  • Each player against themselves. Comparing you to your own baseline means "worse players lose more" can't explain the result.
  • Early-game performance. Gold lead at 14 minutes, measured before most of a game is decided.
  • Selection handled. Players who stop after a loss aren't the same as players who keep queueing, and the analysis accounts for it.
  • Null results count. If loser's queue isn't real, that's the finding.
