Ekko: four candidate worlds

Each world is one idea that sets the mark, the palette, the type and the motion. The mark in every card is the same: EKKO with the first K mirrored, so the two K's sit back to back like a signal going out and its echo coming back.

EKKO
01 · Signal

The lab instrument

An oscilloscope in a dark room. A loss is a pulse, and the trace shows whether it rings on or dies out. A damped wave is literally the cooldown hypothesis (H1b).
